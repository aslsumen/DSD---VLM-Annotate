# Meeting Minutes

**No minutes are recorded in this repository.** The team has not supplied meeting records, and the harness
cannot attend meetings. Nothing here should be inferred as a meeting that took place.

## Rules

1. Minutes are written by the person who chaired or a nominated note-taker, within 24 hours.
2. They contain: date/time, attendees **who actually attended**, agenda, decisions, actions with owner and
   date, and links to evidence.
3. Never summarise a discussion you did not hear. If you reconstruct from chat, label it as such.
4. Course feedback from supervisors or the customer is recorded verbatim where possible, with the date.

## Naming

```text
docs/meeting-minutes/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md
```

```markdown
# Minutes — <topic> — YYYY-MM-DD

**Time:** HH:MM–HH:MM <timezone> · **Location:** <site/online>
**Chair:** <role> · **Note-taker:** <role>

## Attendees
- <role> (present)
- <role> (apologies)

## Agenda
1. …

## Decisions

|---|---|---|

## Actions

```
